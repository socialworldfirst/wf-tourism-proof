The verdict

Yes, but read the caveats

WorldFirst has tourism customer proof. It is thin, it is British, and most of it is no longer online. Here is the honest shape of it before anyone puts it in front of the Indonesia website team.

What is live and named today

Grab, Singapore. A written case study about Grab's corporate travel payments arm, published on 14 site versions including the Indonesia site. No quote, no named spokesperson, no film. WorldFirst tags it Digitalization and GIG-Economy, not tourism.

What is live but anonymous

Two unnamed customer stories on the Airline and Travel Agency vertical page: a Hong Kong airline with more than 20 years, and a leading Chinese online travel platform. Both deliberately unnamed. No quotes. Live in seven APAC locales, including Indonesia.

What is named and quotable, but offline

Seven UK tourism businesses with verbatim quotes from named people, lifted from a February 2021 article and two older testimonial pages. Every one of those URLs is dead now. The words survive in our own archive. Permission was never cleared on any of them.

What does not exist

No tourism customer story, quote or film from Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam or the Philippines. Not one. The customer story library has no Indonesia file at all. The Canton Fair 2026 interviews contain zero tourism businesses.
